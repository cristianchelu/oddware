# Rev B design review against the part datasheets

**Status:** every must-fix and should-fix item below was applied in commits
6c311cf and 153f7e5 (2026-10-22); the fix for item 3 was to keep R3 and
correct the documented current to 260 mA. The firmware notes are in the
README's Power section.

Reviewed 2026-10-22, schematic as generated by `hardware/tools/design.py`
(netlist in `hardware/zigbee_toy/netlist_intent.txt`). Every IC pin map was
checked against its datasheet pin table, and every subcircuit against the
datasheet's typical application circuit and application notes. Copper was not
reviewed beyond the placement distances the datasheets care about.

Datasheets used: TI DRV8213 (SLVSGV9, in `docs/`), XySemi XB5353A (in
`docs/`), Espressif ESP32-C6-MINI-1/1U v1.5 and the ESP32-C6 hardware design
guidelines, Richtek RT9080 DS9080-05, Top Power TP4054, ST USBLC6-2, AOS
AO3401A / AO3400A, MSKSEMI LIS2DW12TR-MS (LCSC C50385411), Epson FC-135,
B5819W, Alps SKRTLAE010.

## Verdict

No pin-map errors. The accelerometer wiring, which was the biggest unknown,
matches the MSKSEMI datasheet exactly. Three things need a change before
ordering; the rest are notes for firmware or for the next revision.

## Must fix

### 1. USBLC6 VBUS pin back-feeds from the USB D+ pull-up (U7 pin 5)

The USBLC6-2SC6 is a pair of diodes from each data line to its VBUS pin
(pin 5) plus a TVS from VBUS to GND. Pin 5 is on the VBUS net. The ESP32-C6
USB Serial/JTAG PHY enables its D+ pull-up from ROM boot on (that is why a
blank module enumerates), so with the cable unplugged D+ sits at 3.3 V while
the VBUS net is at 0 V. The D+ to VBUS diode then conducts and charges the
VBUS net up to roughly 3.3 V minus a diode drop.

Consequences:

- VBUS_SENSE reads about 0.6 × 2.8 V ≈ 1.7 V, which is between VIL (0.83 V)
  and VIH (2.5 V) on the C6. "Plugged in" detection becomes unreliable
  while unplugged.
- The leak feeds the VBUS divider (84 kΩ, ~30 µA) and the TP4054 VCC pin,
  whose datasheet gives 45 µA typical when VCC is below VBAT. That is
  ~75 µA pulled from the 3V3 rail whenever the D+ pull-up is on, which is
  more than the whole idle budget in the README.

Fix: connect U7 pin 5 to 3V3 instead of VBUS (one net change in
`design.py`: `'5': '3V3'`). The C6 PHY is 3.3 V signalling, so clamping
D+/D− to 3V3 is the right reference anyway. VBUS itself keeps C4 as its
transient absorber. The alternative is firmware that disables the D+ pull-up
whenever VBUS_SENSE is low, which ESP-IDF does not do by default.

### 2. CHG_STAT cannot be read with the C6's internal pull-up (U4 pin 1)

The TP4054 CHRG pin has three states: strong pull-down (~10 mA) while
charging, weak pull-down of 8–35 µA (20 µA typical) when VCC is present and
the charge has terminated, and high impedance when VCC is absent. The design
relies on the C6's internal weak pull-up, which the module datasheet gives as
45 kΩ typical.

20 µA × 45 kΩ = 0.9 V, so "charge done" puts the pin at about 2.4 V typical
and 1.7 V worst case, both below the C6's VIH of 2.5 V. The MCU will read
"charging" after termination.

Fix: add a 10 kΩ pull-up from 3V3 to CHG_STAT. The weak pull-down then
drops at most 0.35 V (pin at ≥ 2.95 V, a clean high). It costs 330 µA only
while actually charging, and nothing when unplugged (CHRG is high-Z then).
Done-versus-unplugged is told apart by VBUS_SENSE.

### 3. Charge current is 260 mA, not 300 mA (R3)

The TP4054 datasheet uses two formulas. `R_PROG = 1000 / I_BAT` only holds
up to 150 mA. Above that the datasheet's formula one applies:

    R_PROG = (1000 / I_BAT) × (1.2 − 4 I_BAT / 3)

which gives 1.66 kΩ for 400 mA (matching the electrical table) and 260 mA
for 3.3 kΩ. The schematic note and the README say 300 mA. Either accept
260 mA (0.5 C for the bird cell, fine) and correct the text, or use 2.7 kΩ
for 300 mA. Termination current is a tenth of the programmed value.

## Should fix (cheap, next spin)

- **100 nF at module pin 3.** The module datasheet's peripheral schematic
  puts 10 µF plus 0.1 µF at 3V3. C9 (10 µF) is there; the nearest 100 nF
  caps are the accelerometer's. Add one next to pin 3.
- **External pull-up on IO9 (BOOT).** The C6 design guidelines recommend
  an external pull-up on GPIO9 rather than relying on the internal 45 kΩ.
  10 kΩ to 3V3. Keep the pin free of capacitance, as the guidelines ask.
- **Accelerometer pad 4 to GND.** The MSKSEMI pin table lists pin 4 as NC
  but its connection column says GND in every interface mode. The design
  leaves it open. Tie it to ground to match the datasheet.
- **Motor connector rating vs the 1.66 A limit.** The JST-SH is 1 A per
  pin and both pins carry the motor current. GAINSEL low (1.66 A) exceeds
  that during a stall. Either treat the 324 mA setting as the default for
  both motors and reserve the low-gain setting for measurement, or accept
  the overload as brief (the DRV8213 chops at the limit, so it is a
  regulated 1.66 A, not an inrush).
- **TP4054 copper.** The datasheet example assumes 150 °C/W and shows the
  current folding back at about 45 °C ambient for 400 mA. At 260 mA and a
  3.7 V cell the loss is 0.34 W; with a bare SOT-23-5 on this board expect
  foldback somewhere around 35–50 °C ambient. Widen the GND and BAT copper
  at U4 and give it a few vias to the back pour.

## Notes for firmware

- **Low-battery cutoff is firmware's job.** The XB5353A trips at 2.4 V;
  the C6 needs 3.0 V and the RT9080 adds 0.2–0.3 V of dropout at radio
  current. Below about 3.4 V the C6 will brown out during TX bursts and
  reboot in a loop, each boot drawing more current. Sleep indefinitely (or
  stop the radio) at ~3.4 V and let the protection IC be the last line only.
- **Charger never terminates under load.** The TP4054 terminates at 26 mA.
  While USB is plugged in the board draws from VBAT through the charger, so
  with the radio awake the charger stays in CV float and CHRG stays low.
  Harmless for the cell (it sits at 4.2 V), but do not treat "still
  charging" as an error.
- **DRV8213 auto-sleep needs IN1 = IN2 = 0.** Brake (1/1) keeps the driver
  awake. Park the inputs low, not braked, before sleeping.
- **CHRG three-state decode.** With the 10 kΩ pull-up: low = charging,
  high + VBUS_SENSE high = done, high + VBUS_SENSE low = unplugged.
- **IPROPI ADC.** VIPROPI is clamped at VREF = 0.51 V, so use 0 dB
  attenuation (0–1 V range). The datasheet wants 1.25 V of VM headroom
  above VIPROPI; VBAT is always above 1.76 V so that is met.
- **Accelerometer.** Pins match the MSKSEMI datasheet (1 SDO, 2 SDA,
  3 VDDIO, 5 INT1, 6 INT2, 7 VDD, 8 GNDIO, 9 GND, 10 CS, 12 SCL). CS has an
  internal pull-up to VDDIO enabled by default (CS_PU_EN = 0), so I2C mode
  with CS open is per datasheet. SDO open gives address 0x19; the datasheet
  explicitly recommends leaving SDO floating or high because grounding it
  leaks through its pull-up. WHO_AM_I 0x11 at 0x0F; version in register
  0x70. INT1/INT2 default push-pull, active high (INT_PP_OD = 0,
  H_LACTIVE = 0).
